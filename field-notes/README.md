## How these links are maintained

- HonorlyAI articles are the canonical explanatory sources.
- Repository templates remain editable and may be copied under CC BY 4.0.
- Links use descriptive titles rather than repetitive commercial anchor text.
- New links are added only when they help a reader complete a real district task.
- Time-sensitive articles identify their publication and review dates and should be verified against current official sources.

These materials are general educational resources, not legal advice, a compliance certification, or a substitute for district-specific review.
