# Linking, citation, and attribution guide

These resources are designed to be copied and adapted. Clear attribution helps educators locate updates, compare versions, and identify the original source without implying endorsement.

## Suggested attribution for an adaptation

> Adapted from the **HonorlyAI K-12 AI Governance Toolkit**, https://github.com/trevor050/honorlyai-resources. Licensed under CC BY 4.0. Changes were made.

## Suggested citation for a webpage or presentation

> HonorlyAI LLC. *HonorlyAI K-12 AI Governance Toolkit*. Version 1.0.0, 2026. https://github.com/trevor050/honorlyai-resources.

The repository also includes [CITATION.cff](../CITATION.cff) for tools that support Citation File Format.

## What attribution does not mean

Attribution, a link, a fork, a citation, or use of the templates does not mean HonorlyAI has reviewed, approved, certified, or endorsed the adapted policy or the organization using it.

Do not use HonorlyAI's name or logo in a way that implies partnership or approval without written permission.
