## Part E: aggregate first, individual drill-down second

Confirm:

- [ ] Ordinary instructional patterns are shown in aggregate where possible.
- [ ] Individual conversation access requires a defined instructional, safety, integrity, or support purpose.
- [ ] The interface shows the assignment rule and surrounding context.
- [ ] The teacher can inspect the evidence behind a summary or alert.
- [ ] The teacher can dismiss, correct, or annotate inaccurate output.
- [ ] Administrative and vendor access is role-limited and logged.
- [ ] The system does not create a permanent student behavior or risk profile from routine academic questions.
- [ ] Students and families receive plain-language notice of what adults can see.
