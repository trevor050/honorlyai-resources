## 3. Prohibited or deferred uses

The pilot does not authorize:

- final high-stakes decisions about students;
- unreviewed automated discipline or misconduct findings;
- use of personal accounts when district accounts are required;
- collection or upload of data outside the approved data map;
- expansion to additional schools, grades, subjects, or purposes without review;
- `[LOCAL EXCLUSIONS]`.

## 6. Training plan

Training should cover:

- the educational purpose and excluded uses;
- assignment-level AI use directions;
- student disclosure expectations;
- teacher visibility and intervention;
- privacy and data-entry boundaries;
- inaccurate, harmful, or unsafe output;
- accessibility and alternatives;
- incident reporting;
- how product or model changes will be communicated.

## 8. Stop conditions

Pause or stop the pilot when:

- unauthorized access or disclosure may have occurred;
- the product materially changes data use, model behavior, or subprocessors without completed review;
- safeguards cannot support the approved age or use case;
- accessibility barriers lack a workable alternative;
- the vendor cannot meet a material contractual or security requirement;
- staff cannot implement the required oversight;
- evidence indicates educational harm or unacceptable inequity;
- `[LOCAL CONDITION]`.
