# Contributing to the HonorlyAI K-12 AI Governance Toolkit

Thank you for helping make practical AI governance resources easier for schools to use.

## Useful contributions

We especially welcome:

- corrections supported by authoritative sources;
- clearer, plainer language for families and students;
- implementation notes from educators and school leaders;
- accessibility improvements;
- translations with a named reviewer;
- additional state or local review prompts that are clearly labeled and sourced;
- examples that distinguish policy requirements from optional practice;
- formatting that makes the templates easier to adapt.

## Before opening a pull request

1. Open or find an issue describing the problem.
2. Keep the change focused.
3. Preserve editable placeholders such as `[DISTRICT NAME]`.
4. Avoid absolute claims such as "FERPA compliant," "COPPA certified," "bias-free," or "completely secure."
5. Cite primary sources when a statement depends on law, regulation, or formal guidance.
6. Do not include student records, personal information, confidential district material, credentials, private contracts, or unpublished HonorlyAI product information.
7. Run a link check and read the rendered Markdown.

## Writing style

- Put the direct answer or purpose near the top.
- Use descriptive headings that match how educators search for the topic.
- Prefer concrete responsibilities, decisions, and evidence over slogans.
- Define acronyms on first use.
- Separate required local decisions from suggested defaults.
- Write for a mixed audience of educators, administrators, technology staff, families, students, and counsel.
- Use "supports review" or "designed to support" rather than guaranteeing legal compliance.

## Sources

Legal and policy claims should point to official materials whenever possible, including:

- U.S. Department of Education student privacy guidance;
- Federal Trade Commission COPPA guidance;
- NIST AI Risk Management Framework materials;
- applicable state agencies and statutes.

See [Sources and further reading](docs/sources-and-further-reading.md).

## Pull request checklist

- [ ] The title says what changed.
- [ ] The description explains the user problem.
- [ ] All factual claims are supportable.
- [ ] Legal claims are narrowly stated and sourced.
- [ ] No private or identifying information is included.
- [ ] Links work.
- [ ] Headings are descriptive.
- [ ] The resource remains editable.
- [ ] The license and disclaimer remain intact.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under this repository's applicable license.
