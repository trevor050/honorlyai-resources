# Community Code of Conduct

The HonorlyAI K-12 AI Governance Toolkit exists to support careful, practical discussion about technology used by students and schools.

## Our standard

Participants should:

- be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented;
- distinguish disagreement from personal attack;
- protect student, educator, and family privacy;
- avoid stereotyping students, schools, communities, or disability groups;
- welcome corrections and explain reasoning;
- disclose relevant vendor, consulting, or institutional interests;
- keep public discussions free of confidential records and security-sensitive details.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, impersonation, spam, undisclosed promotion, deliberate misinformation, and publication of private or security-sensitive information.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact HonorlyAI through https://honorlyai.com/contact.

For product security or privacy vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not publish details in a public issue.

Project maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that violate this code and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
